    Workfront Planning and Workfront Core do not share one list of data types, so each field has two answers. Planning takes one of Adobe's 17 types. Core needs two values: a dataType and a displayType (the display types SLCT, MULT and CHCK are not yet verified in a sandbox). The tool proposes a type from the default mapping for each Airtable type; you apply the defaults, override them, or clear a value. Every change records who made it, when, and why (the initials and reason above), and the history keeps the old value.
