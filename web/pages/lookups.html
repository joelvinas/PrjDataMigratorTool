    A single or multiple select field with hundreds of options (a list of cities, for example) is not realistic as a select field, and its options often need more data of their own. Here a person can authorize such a field to be migrated as a lookup list instead: a Workfront Planning record type of its own, with one record per option, loaded before the table that uses it. The select field then becomes a one-sided link to that record type. The tool only flags long lists and proposes a name; nothing is decided until a person does it, and every decision keeps who, when and why. Core is not part of this yet. Nothing is sent to Workfront from this page.
